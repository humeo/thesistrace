# Use full-fill Open execution with frozen costs and price slippage

Under the Strategy contract in [ADR-0250](0250-admit-strategy-programs-with-explicit-simulation-coordinates.md), eligible orders fill completely using the next daily Open as reference, subject to Canonical suspension, raw-Open directional price limits and existing quantity and cash constraints. Each run freezes configurable explicit fees and adverse fixed-basis-point price slippage, defaulting to existing fee values and zero slippage; slipped prices affect settlement, fee notional and affordability without a second slippage debit. No additional post-slippage limit clipping or rejection is applied, accepting potentially out-of-bound synthetic prices while excluding order-book queues, partial fills, volume participation and inferred universal price limits.
