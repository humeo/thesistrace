# Let Strategies trigger targets independently of selection schedules

The Strategy contract in [ADR-0250](0250-admit-strategy-programs-with-explicit-simulation-coordinates.md) places Rebalance Triggers in the selected Strategy, allowing opportunities, signals and account conditions to produce a final Target Decision without passing a global Selection Interval gate. The built-in periodic policy retains its original selection phase and independent exposure changes: reductions scale Actual Holdings proportionally, while an explicit increase may use retained selection. Daily evaluation, price drift, retained targets or the lifting of a risk restriction alone do not replay allocations, preserving no-update behavior without restricting every Strategy to scheduled selection.
