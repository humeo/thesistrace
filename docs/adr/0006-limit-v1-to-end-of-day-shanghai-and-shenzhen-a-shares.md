# Limit V1 to end-of-day Shanghai and Shenzhen A-shares

ThesisTrace V1 supports daily, post-close research for ordinary
CNY-denominated A-shares on the SSE Main Board, SZSE Main Board, ChiNext, and
STAR Market. ADR-0075 defines the point-in-time Universe Base Pool and excluded
instrument types. Other markets, other asset classes, intraday data, real-time
research, and live trading are outside the V1 boundary so data availability,
factor evaluation, and execution assumptions can share one end-of-day market
model.
