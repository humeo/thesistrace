# Use user-selected Research Periods and complete dependency warm-up

A ResearchRun freezes the requested natural-date range, maps it to Research Sessions, and derives Calculation Warm-up from every dependency required by its Research Kind, including Alpha and Exposure expressions, shared indicators, Portfolio Weighting and declared Strategy dependencies where applicable. One combined dependency plan retains each expression's own meaning while preventing a valid Signal window from hiding missing history required by the portfolio rules. Warm-up never enters reported results, the period is never shifted to hide missing history or right-censoring, and publication fails rather than truncating required output.
