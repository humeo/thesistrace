# Use an independent append-only CSI 300 Benchmark Snapshot

Strategy Comparison aligns Strategy facts with one independent append-only CSI 300 Price Index Open history over the same investable interval. Benchmark coverage must be sufficient before Market publication, so the benchmark may lead a failed data publication but the Dataset Head cannot lead its benchmark. Keeping benchmark history outside immutable Research Results allows comparisons to fail independently of Strategy execution while rejecting missing levels rather than substituting another index or a carried price.
