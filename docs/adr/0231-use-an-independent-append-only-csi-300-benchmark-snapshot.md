# Use an independent append-only CSI 300 Benchmark Snapshot

Strategy Comparison uses the ordinary CSI 300 Price Index Open from the first investable Entry Open through Terminal Valuation, sourced from one independently published append-only Benchmark Snapshot rather than Canonical Data, Product State, or immutable Results. Dataset Bootstrap and Market Refresh must publish sufficient Benchmark Coverage before advancing Market Head, accepting that the Snapshot may lead a failed publication but never allowing Market Head to lead it. Strategy execution and Tracking retain only Strategy facts and remain available when comparison is unavailable, while missing or invalid Benchmark Levels block Market publication instead of invoking carry, correction, an alternate index, or a remote fallback.
