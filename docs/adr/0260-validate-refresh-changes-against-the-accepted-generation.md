# Validate Refresh changes against the accepted Generation

Data Refresh validates changed Market session blocks against the exact accepted predecessor, reuses its unchanged immutable partitions, and verifies Financial statements and indicators through their existing incremental source-projection validators. Composition still checks Family references, Coverage, calendar coordinates, source identities and fields; publication retains its ownership guard and atomic Dataset Head comparison. Retry reconstructs the same incremental validation against its saved predecessor rather than trusting a persisted validation flag. This replaces the complete historical revalidation requirement of ADR-0155 and refines ADR-0254 without permitting partial publication. Untouched historical files are verified when read; `thesistrace-data-operator audit-generation --generation-manifest-sha256 <sha256>` performs a separate full stored-history audit without moving Dataset Head or fetching suppliers. Full audit retains deterministic Parquet encoding and historical source checks, trading per-refresh historical damage discovery for work bounded by changed business data and small metadata.
