# Keep post-Open performance separate from Close risk valuation

Each primary Strategy observation, including the last Research Period Session, accrues holdings to the current Open, executes due decisions from completed prior Sessions, applies costs and records post-trade Gross and Net NAV. Period end causes no forced liquidation, and its final Close decision remains pending for a later Open, allowing tracking to continue without replacing prior observations. The next Strategy contract in [ADR-0250](0250-admit-strategy-programs-with-explicit-simulation-coordinates.md) adds separately named Close Risk NAV and risk-cycle state without redefining the primary performance series, benchmark coordinates or historical drawdown.
