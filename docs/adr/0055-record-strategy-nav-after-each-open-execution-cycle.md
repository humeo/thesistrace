# Record Strategy NAV after each Open execution cycle

Each Strategy observation, including the last Research Period Session, accrues prior holdings to the current Open, executes due decisions from completed prior Sessions, applies costs, and records post-trade Gross and Net NAV. The period end causes no forced liquidation; a decision formed after its final Close remains pending for a later Open rather than making the published final observation provisional. Treating the last included Session like any other completed Session allows tracking to continue without replacing prior observations, deliberately changing the former valuation-only endpoint contract.
