# Make batch-versus-incremental equivalence the V1 end-to-end goal

V1's end-to-end correctness goal is that a Research Definition produces canonically equivalent Factor and Strategy results through reference execution and session-by-session DailyTrack execution from the same Tracking Origin, ordered Research Sessions, pinned contracts, and Canonical inputs. The platform verifies this through explicit equivalence verification rather than replaying the complete history during every ordinary Advance.
