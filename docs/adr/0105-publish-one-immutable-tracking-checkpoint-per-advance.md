# Publish one immutable Tracking Checkpoint per Advance

A successful Tracking Advance atomically publishes one immutable Checkpoint bound to its predecessor, frozen target sessions, Data Generation, contracts, bounded results, and completed terminal Strategy state, including already-decided targets awaiting a future Open. Successors add later Sessions without replacing a predecessor's boundary observation or reconstructing its decisions from revised historical data. The Tracking Head moves only after complete publication, and failed Attempts expose no partial progress or cross-Attempt checkpoint.
