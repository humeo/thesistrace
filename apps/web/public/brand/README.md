# Quantgrove brand assets

- `quantgrove-mark.svg` is the vector master based on the user-approved folded-leaf/open-book direction in [the brand board](../../../../docs/design/quantgrove-brand-reference.png).
- `quantgrove-mark-inverse.svg` is the inverse mark for the existing dark console.
- `quantgrove-lockup.svg` and its inverse contain the mark and an outlined Source Serif 4 wordmark, independent of installed fonts. The font's [SIL OFL license](../fonts/sourceserif4-OFL.txt) is included.
- `quantgrove-icon.svg` places the white mark on forest green. PNG exports at 32, 128 and 180px serve favicons, email and touch icons.
- `quantgrove-botanical.jpg` is the approved generated prototype photograph (570 × 760, approximately 101 KiB). It is decorative and has empty alt text in the hero.
- `../quanttrace-logo.png` remains available for previously sent email references. New UI and email use Quantgrove assets.

Production font sources and licenses are documented in [fonts/README.md](../fonts/README.md).

## QAgentis — E1-B

The QAgentis SVG masters implement the E1-B silhouette with the user's 2026-10-07 refinement: two equal 27 × 29 radius ellipses centered at (35, 36) and (61, 36) in a 96 × 72 viewBox. Both centers share the same horizontal line, replacing the previous 7-unit vertical offset. Their overlap remains transparent through even-odd fill; its width at the midpoint increases from about 22.6 to 28 units (about 24%). The mark preserves its 4:3 canvas ratio. PNG app icons at 32, 128 and 180px are exports of qhypo-icon.svg. The React Brand component renders QAgentis (uppercase Q and A) in locally hosted Inter, weight 550 and -.02em tracking, matching the former QuantTrace homepage wordmark. The website domain is qhypo.com; it is not part of the wordmark. This refinement supersedes the offset silhouette in docs/design/qhypo-brand-reference.png. See fonts/README.md for font provenance.
